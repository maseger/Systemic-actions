The ideaTanken

You cannot develop leaders out of a system that keeps producing the same behaviour. Man kan inte utveckla ledare ur ett system som fortsätter att skapa samma beteenden.

For more than twenty years I worked in leadership development. From 2012 I began testing new approaches in government agencies, state-owned companies and listed companies, and one lesson kept returning: the diagnosis is systemic, but the efforts often stop at the individual and the group.

Systemic Actions is what I learned along the way. It is systems thinking for leaders who have to act on Monday morning: how to tell the complicated from the complex, where the leverage points are, which structures you are prepared to change, and how what your leaders discover can reach the people who can change the conditions.

I mer än tjugo år arbetade jag med ledarutveckling. Från 2012 började jag pröva nya grepp i myndigheter, statliga bolag och börsnoterade bolag, och en lärdom kom tillbaka gång på gång: diagnosen är systemisk, men insatserna stannar ofta på individ- och gruppnivå.

Systemic Actions är det jag lärde mig på vägen. Det är systemtänkande för ledare som ska agera på måndag morgon: hur du skiljer det komplicerade från det komplexa, var hävstängerna finns, vilka strukturer du är beredd att ändra och hur det dina ledare upptäcker kan nå dem som kan ändra förutsättningarna.

How your email address is handled

Mattias Seger is the data controller. I process your address on the basis of your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

When you ask for material, I send one email asking you to confirm your address. Nothing is stored until you confirm. Once you do, you receive the material, four short emails over two weeks, and then news when there is something worth saying. You can unsubscribe at any time with one click. The material is also available directly from the page after you submit the form.

Your address is stored with Resend, the delivery service that sends the emails. Resend is a US company, so a transfer outside the EU/EEA can occur. The emails are sent through Resend's EU region (Ireland), and I do not track opens or clicks. I keep your address until you unsubscribe or ask me to delete it.

You can ask to see, correct or delete what I hold about you by writing to mattias@systemicactions.com, and you can complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY). The page remembers your language choice in your browser. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics; the material opens on Google Drive, which has its own terms.
